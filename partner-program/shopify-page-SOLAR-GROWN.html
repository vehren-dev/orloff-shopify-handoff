The process

Sunlight in, diamond out.

A diamond takes millions of years to form underground. Ours take a few weeks, in a lab powered by more than 100 acres of solar panels.

The method

Chemical vapor deposition. Carbon builds up layer by layer under conditions that mirror what happens naturally, just compressed and controlled.

The energy

The facility where the stones are grown runs on more than 100 acres of solar panels, which is the part most growers will not put a number on.

The result

Pure carbon. Chemically, physically and optically identical to a diamond pulled out of the ground, and graded by the same laboratory.
