# Outreach sequence: Nordic independent jewellers

Four emails. Send days 1, 4, 9, 16. **Stop the sequence on any reply.**

Merge fields: `[Name]` `[Shop name]`
Confirm-before-sending fields are marked `[CONFIRM: …]` / `[BEKRÄFTA: …]`.

Written short and plain on purpose. The recipient is a working jeweller who gets
supplier pitches weekly and deletes the ones that open with a brand story.

**Before the first send:** confirm you have a lawful basis to email these
businesses. B2B cold email to a company address is generally permissible under
GDPR on legitimate interests, but you must include a working opt-out and honour
it immediately, and the ePrivacy rules on electronic marketing are transposed
differently across the Nordics. Worth ten minutes with whoever handles your
compliance before a list send.
