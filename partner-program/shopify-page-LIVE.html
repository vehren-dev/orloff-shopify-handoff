The presentation

The box is part of the argument.

Every ring is presented in a turned beech wood box with the Orloff wordmark burned into the lid, lined with natural linen rather than the moulded foam and flocked plastic the trade normally uses.

Beech is Denmark's national tree. Linen is a plant fibre. Neither needs a disclaimer, which is rather the point. A customer who opens that box has already understood what kind of company made the ring inside it.
