Sunlight in, diamond out

The Process

A diamond is carbon, pressure, and time. We simply changed where the time is spent.

A diamond takes millions of years to form underground. Ours take a few weeks, in a lab powered by more than 100 acres of solar panels. The process is called chemical vapor deposition, carbon building up layer by layer under conditions that mirror what happens naturally, just compressed and controlled.
