================================================================
METHOD
================================================================

poppler was not installed and could not be, but `pdftotext` is already
present on this machine via Git for Windows (Xpdf 4.06 build). No install
was required.

`-layout`, which the job doc mandates, is NOT usable on this document.
Under -layout the row-number/model column, the weight/qty column and the
price column are three INDEPENDENT vertical sequences that share physical
lines by accident. On page 3, rows 19 and 21 carry numbers on their own
line while rows 20 and 22 carry none. The job doc's anchoring rule
("everything from one row-number match until the next is one record")
would have silently misassigned prices by one row across large parts of
the document, and on page 37 weight/qty and unit/total are on separate
lines from each other as well.

`pdftotext -table` resolves this completely: it emits one clean stanza
per record with row number, model number, weight, qty, unit and total all
on a single physical line. The parser is built on -table output and
anchors on "Material" labels, of which there are exactly 554.

Model numbers split across two lines (rows 460, 464, 513-521, 523-524,
531, 532 and others) are reassembled by concatenating the left-column
region of every line in the stanza. Example: row 532 prints "OR" on one
line and "532  GCB182-2" on the next, giving ORGCB182-2.

                     Neighbouring row 248 ORCB183-S is 4.00 x 10 = 40.00
                     exactly, so 40.00 appears to have been carried across.

                     4.22 x 10 = 42.20. Invoice states 42.22. Delta +0.02.
                     Rows 249, 251 and 253 are all 4.22 x 10 = 42.20,
                     so 42.22 is a single-digit typo.

Combined overstatement: +0.06. The invoice grand total of US$55,849.61
therefore includes 6 cents that unit x qty does not support. True value
by unit x qty is US$55,849.55. Nothing has been adjusted.

Both failing rows are ORCB183 letters, which ARE in scope for Line A.
